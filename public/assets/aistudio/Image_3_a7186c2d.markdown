## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a serene, compassionate, and refined editorial atmosphere tailored for families curating memorial arrangements, keepsakes, and floral tributes in honor of Margaret Williams (1942–2026). It reconciles the emotional tenderness of mourning with the clarity and dignity of an artisanal boutique catalog.

### Emotional Demeanor & Persona
The experience rejects transactional aggression, urgent count-down badges, and loud merchandising cues. Instead, the interface acts as an empathetic steward: composed, reverent, spacious, and patient. Every visual decision reinforces reassurance, enduring love, and quiet elegance. Subtle references to the partner institution—Willow & Pine Funeral Home—are integrated through quiet, dignified crests and micro-labels rather than overt commercial headers.

### Visual Language: Editorial Elegance & Subtle Botanicals
- **Whitespace as Comfort:** Generous canvas margins and intentional pauses between sections provide breathing room for emotionally taxed users.
- **Hairline Restraint:** Delicate 1px dividers, reminiscent of fine stationery and letterpress paper, organize content without harsh grid divisions.
- **Artisanal Typography:** High-contrast editorial display serifs evoke heirloom literature, balanced with humanist sans-serifs that maintain unwavering legibility for older or distressed family members.
- **Soft Tactility:** Photography carries soft film grain, natural diffuse lighting, warm ivory undertones, and organic botanical motifs (willow leaves, wild pine, gentle flora).

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from dried flora, natural unbleached linen, and weathered stone. Rather than traditional stark funeral black, deep charcoal anchors typography and critical interactive states with gentle authority.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#343835` — Deep Forest Charcoal):** Replaces harsh jet-black. Used for editorial headlines, key CTAs, and structural grounding.
- **Secondary (`#899588` — Muted Sage):** Evokes quiet nature, fresh arrangements, and restorative peace. Applied to category badges, selected pills, and subtle iconography.
- **Tertiary (`#A58A6D` — Muted Bronze / Warm Sand):** Imparts warmth, enduring craft, and bespoke craftsmanship. Used for keepsake accents, price typography, personalization badges, and commemorative dates.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8F6F1` — Soft Alabaster Ivory):** The primary surface color, providing an organic, paper-like warmth that softens screen glare.
- **Elevated White (`#FFFFFF` — Warm White):** Reserved for card containers, inputs, and elevated memorial frames against the ivory base.
- **Delicate Border (`#E6E1D7` — Pale Linen):** A soft, low-contrast boundary tone used for 1px hairline dividers and subtle element outlines.

### Application Rules
Never employ saturated status alerts (e.g., bright electric green or emergency red). Feedback uses deep sage for confirmation and a gentle terracotta rose (`#B86B64`) for form guidance, always accompanied by clear, compassionate copy.

## Typography

The typographic pairing merges literary heirloom grace with absolute accessibility.

- **Playfair Display (Display & Headlines):** Used for Margaret Williams’ memorial dedication, section headers, artisan product titles, and thoughtful pull quotes. It communicates permanence, reverence, and human history. It should never be rendered in heavy weights; 400 and 500 weights preserve delicate serifs.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & Micro-copy):** A geometric sans-serif softened with humanist details. Its wide aperture and sturdy counters offer strain-free reading for long personalization descriptions, shipping specifics, and floral arrangement dimensions.
- **Commemorative Numerals & Dates:** Life years (e.g., *1942 – 2026*) use `Playfair Display` with deliberate word spacing and en-dashes to honor the memorial milestone.
- **Letterpress Uppercase:** Categories and provenance labels (`label-xs`, `label-md`) use uppercase formatting with open letter-spacing (`0.04em`–`0.08em`) to mirror archival collection stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an editorial grid reminiscent of a high-end monograph or fine art exhibition catalogue. It combines structured product alignment with generous, breathing white spaces that prevent visual fatigue.

### Grid Rhythm & Columns
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with max-width container capped at `1280px` to maintain comfortable eye movement. Gutter size defaults to `gutter-lg` (`2.5rem`), and horizontal page margins scale to `margin-lg` (`4rem`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `gutter` (`1.5rem`) and `margin-md` (`2.5rem`).
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout with `gutter` (`1.0rem`) and outer canvas padding locked at `margin` (`1.25rem`).

### Rhythm Principles
- Memorial banners and curated collections should incorporate `space-xl` breathing room above and below section dividers to prevent items from feeling congested.
- Two-column asymmetrical arrangements (e.g., 7 columns for artisanal imagery paired with 5 columns for bespoke personalization controls) are preferred over dense, repetitive multi-card matrices.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain a serene, grounded aesthetic, depth relies primarily on tonal surfaces and delicate tactile framing rather than stacked drop shadows or floating 3D layers.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8F6F1` (Soft Ivory).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards, Selectors):** Pure `#FFFFFF` (Warm White) framed by a 1px hairline border of `#E6E1D7`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Drawers, Memorial Lightboxes):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-diffuse ambient shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(52, 56, 53, 0.04)`. The shadow is tinted with the charcoal base, evoking the soft falloff of natural morning window light.

### Hairlines & Boundaries
Rather than thick containment boxes, sections breathe across horizontal and vertical divider lines set to `1px solid #E6E1D7`. Botanical watermarks or subtle leaf crest accents can be embedded into central divider intersections for an artisanal touch.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, restrained geometry (`roundedness: 1`). Softened corners evoke fine cardstock, archival photo mounts, and hand-bound journals.

### Radius Scale
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Cards):** `0.25rem` (4px). Avoid completely square edges which can feel clinical or punitive, as well as bubbly pill shapes which diminish formal reverence.
- **Large Framing (Memorial Hero Portraits, Keepsake Previews):** `0.5rem` (8px). Softens framing around portrait photography without distorting the composition.
- **Arch Motifs:** Specific featured collections (e.g., "Floral Tributes", "Commemorative Urns") may incorporate classical arched top corners (`border-top-left-radius: 9999px; border-top-right-radius: 9999px`) on imagery, echoing historic garden gates and memorial architecture.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Memorial Action (e.g., "Personalize Keepsake", "Send to Service"):** Background in `#343835` (Deep Charcoal) with `#F8F6F1` text, 4px border radius, vertical padding `0.875rem`, horizontal padding `1.75rem`. Hover smoothly shifts to `#4A504B` with zero elevation jump.
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "View Tribute Details"):** 1px border of `#B7A99A` (Taupe) over transparent canvas, text in `#343835`. Hover warms the background to `#F2EFEA`.
- **Compassionate Ghost Buttons:** Understated text buttons with an animated 1px underline that gracefully expands from center upon hover.

### Product & Tribute Cards
- Built on a clean `#FFFFFF` container with a 1px border of `#E6E1D7`.
- Images use natural aspect ratios (4:5 or 3:4) with subtle ivory undertones.
- Card content hierarchy:
  1. Provenance / Category in `label-xs` (`#899588`, uppercase tracking).
  2. Tribute Name in `headline-sm` (`#343835`).
  3. Pricing & Crafting notes in `body-sm` (`#A58A6D`).
  4. Subtle inclusion indicator: "Delivery coordinated with Willow & Pine Funeral Home".

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Non-intrusive 28px height chips with a 4px corner radius.
- Inactive: `#F8F6F1` background, 1px border `#E6E1D7`, text `#343835`.
- Active: `#899588` (Muted Sage) background, `#FFFFFF` text, no border.

### Input Fields & Personalization Modules
- Text inputs and condolence engraving areas use `#FFFFFF` backgrounds, `0.25rem` radius, and 1px border `#D8D1C5`.
- Focused state transitions to a 1px border of `#899588` with a soft 2px outer aura in `rgba(137, 149, 136, 0.2)`. Never use harsh blue outlines.
- Character counters for inscriptions (e.g., "Margaret Williams: Cherished Mother & Grandmother") are displayed in `label-xs` taupe.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Radio markers use concentric circular rings: 18px diameter, `#E6E1D7` resting border, filled with a 10px `#899588` inner disc when selected.
- Checkboxes use a 4px soft square with `#899588` background and a pure white `#FFFFFF` refined checkmark.

### Bespoke Funeral Home Attributions
- Quiet micro-badges featuring a thin line-art pine needle or willow sprig icon accompanied by the text: *"Curated in partnership with Willow & Pine Funeral Home"*, set in `label-md` `#A58A6D`.
